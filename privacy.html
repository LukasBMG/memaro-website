2. In short

  • Without an account, Memaro stores no game results, statistics or profile details – neither on your device nor on our servers.
  • With an account, your training data is stored and synced between your devices. Servers are located in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking or analytics services.
  • Other players never see your individual results, name, age or gender.

3. Using Memaro without an account

All free games can be played without an account. Only device settings (language, sound, vibration) are stored on your device. Results, daily plan progress and statistics are kept in memory only until you close the app.

So that purchases work technically, the app connects to RevenueCat at launch (see section 7) using a random, anonymous identifier. Purchasing is not possible without an account.

4. Account and sign-in

For an account we process:

  • Email sign-up: email address and password (stored only as a hash). We email you a code to confirm your address and to reset your password.
  • Sign in with Apple: an identifier from Apple, your email address (or an Apple relay address) and, if you agree, your name.
  • Sign in with Google: an identifier from Google, your email address and the name of your Google account.
  • Profile: name (optional), year of birth, gender, exercises per day, training goals, favourites and app language.
  • Training data: results of each round (game, date, points, performance, accuracy, speed, language), daily plans and IQ estimate results.
  • Technical data: sign-in and sync times and IP addresses in server logs.

Legal basis: performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Age and gender are used solely to compare you with similar groups of players.

Supabase: accounts, authentication and database are operated for us by Supabase Inc. (USA) as a processor. Data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany (AWS region eu-central-1). Access from third countries for maintenance cannot be ruled out and is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. A data processing agreement is in place.

Resend: confirmation and password codes are sent via Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA) from noreply@mail.memaro.eu using its EU region (Ireland). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Apple and Google also process data under their own privacy policies when you sign in with them.

5. Comparisons with other players

After a game, signed-in players see how their result compares with others. Once an hour the server combines all accounts' results into anonymous distributions per game, age group and gender; each account counts with the average of its last 20 rounds. Groups with fewer than 20 players are not stored. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in offering meaningful comparisons (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object at any time by email.

6. Memaro Plus (subscription)

Memaro Plus requires a Memaro account. Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive payment details such as card numbers.

7. Purchase management: RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as a processor to verify and manage subscriptions. Processed data: an app user ID (your Memaro user ID with an account, an anonymous ID without), store purchase and subscription information, device type, operating system, app version, country/store region and IP address. Training results, name, age or gender are not sent to RevenueCat. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Support requests

When you email us, we process your email address, your message and any details you choose to send in order to handle your request. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail, Google Ireland Limited). Please never send passwords or payment details.

9. This website

memaro.eu is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time and requested page in server logs to deliver the site and prevent abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website uses no cookies, no external fonts and no tracking.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Account and training data are kept until you delete your account in the app (Profile → Account → “Delete account”). This deletes account, profile, results, plans and IQ results on the server and the device, and the purchase link at RevenueCat.
  • “Reset training” deletes results, plans and streak; the account remains.
  • Signing out removes the copy on your device; your account keeps the data.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their legal obligations. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription – cancel it in your store account.
  • Support emails are deleted when no longer needed, at the latest after three years unless a legal retention duty applies.

11. Children

Users under 16 need the consent of a parent or guardian to create an account. Parents can contact us at any time to have their child's data deleted.

12. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact us at memaro.support@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

13. Security

All connections between the app, servers and services are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). On the server, each account can only access its own data.

14. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
